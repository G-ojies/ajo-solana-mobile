Ajo

on Solana Mobile

The rotating savings circle your family already trusts, with the pot held by a Solana program instead of a person.

  1. Agree the rules. Amount, number of people, how often.
  2. Everyone pays in from their phone wallet, straight into the circle's vault.
  3. One person collects the whole pot each round, in an order drawn by code anyone can check.
  4. Nobody can hold the circle up. After a grace period the round goes ahead and a missed payment is on record.
