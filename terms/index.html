How circles work

A circle's rules (amount, members, round length, late fee and grace period) are set when it is created and enforced by the on-chain program. Payout order is drawn by the program when the circle starts. After the grace period a round can be paid out even if a member has not paid, and that missed payment stays on the member's public record. Ajo does not guarantee that other members will pay.
